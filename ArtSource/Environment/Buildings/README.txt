ReGenesis Smart City Building Kit

BUILDINGS
Residential Small: 4 x 4 meter footprint, approximately 5 meters tall.
Office Medium: 4 x 4 meter footprint, approximately 7.5 meters tall.
Utility Service: 4 x 4 meter footprint, approximately 3.6 meters tall.
Landmark Tall: 4 x 4 meter footprint, approximately 10.2 meters tall.
Rooftop Modules: HVAC, energy relay and holographic sign as separate children.

All building roots export at world origin. The overview Blender file arranges
them only for inspection. Snap building roots to the same 4-meter grid used by
the road kit. Rotate buildings in 90-degree increments for variation.

Armor, windows, lights, doors, vents, balconies and roof components are separate
named meshes. Each mesh has an independent UV_Texture map packed in 0-1.
UVs overlap between different objects. Texture separately or repack each building
before baking a shared atlas. Materials are editable colors and emission only.

FBX exports Y up and -Z forward. Buildings are background scenery: interiors,
colliders, LODs, occlusion setup, destructibility, signs and Unity prefabs are
not included.
